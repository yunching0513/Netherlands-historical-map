Theater Tuschinski / Tuschinski Theater

土辛斯基劇院 · Amsterdam, the Netherlands
1918–1921 Amsterdam School / Art Deco Reguliersbreestraat 26–28, Amsterdam

NL

Theater Tuschinski (1918–1921), in opdracht van de Pools-Joodse immigrant Abraham Tuschinski, ontworpen door H.L. de Jong met interieurs van Jaap Gidding, combineert Amsterdamse School-expressionisme met art-decomotieven. De weelderige foyer — glas-in-lood, Perzische tapijten, gouden tinten — maakt het tot Amsterdam's meest luxueuze bioscoop, die nog steeds in gebruik is.

Architect: H.L. de Jong · Stijl: Amsterdamse School / art deco

EN

Theater Tuschinski (1918–1921), commissioned by Polish-Jewish immigrant Abraham Tuschinski and designed by H.L. de Jong with interiors by Jaap Gidding, blends Amsterdam School expressionist brickwork with Art Deco geometry. Its opulent lobby — stained glass, Persian carpets, gilded tones — makes it Amsterdam's most lavish cinema, still in operation today.

Architect: H.L. de Jong · Style: Amsterdam School / Art Deco

中文

土辛斯基劇院（1918–1921）由波蘭移民亞伯拉罕・土辛斯基出資興建，建築師希曼・路易・德容設計，室內裝飾由賈普・吉丁主持。外觀融合阿姆斯特丹學派的表現主義磚牆與裝飾藝術的幾何細節；富麗堂皇的大廳以彩繪玻璃、波斯地毯與黃金色調聞名。是阿姆斯特丹最華麗的電影院，迄今仍在使用。

建築師：希曼・路易・德容 · 風格：阿姆斯特丹學派／裝飾藝術
